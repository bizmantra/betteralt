 How to install 
 Download the ZIP file and unzip it.
 Open your browser’s Extensions page: chrome://extensions or edge://extensions .
 Turn on Developer mode , choose Load unpacked , and select the unzipped Hikaku folder containing manifest.json .
 Firefox
 For desktop Firefox 140 or later.
 Download for Firefox
 Firefox installation options
 Version 0.1.0 is an early release, installed manually. The visual model downloads approximately 199–393 MB on first use, then stays cached in your browser. The popup shows download progress.
 Your images stay on your device.
 Images and video frames are checked in your browser. Hikaku has no accounts, analytics, or browsing-history collection. You don’t need to send your photos to a detection service.
 Hikaku fetches media from the site you’re already viewing. Detection models download from Hugging Face once and are cached locally. Your browsing data is not included with that download.

 What the results can tell you
 Hikaku checks both file metadata and the image itself. Metadata declarations are editable, and a model’s score isn’t a probability that an image is AI-generated. Neither check can give a reliable answer for every image.
 Film scans, renders, and product photos can trigger false flags. Video checks are experimental. Face swaps and small edits to real photos are not reliably detected.

 How to contribute
 A few more questions
 How does it check an image? Hikaku reads file metadata such as Content Credentials (C2PA), IPTC source types, and generator tags, then checks eligible images with an on-device visual model. File declarations and model estimates stay separate. A filter skips likely screenshots and flat graphics. Images smaller than 160 pixels on their shortest side are also skipped.
 Will it slow down my browser? The first visual check includes a model download and initialization. After loading, the project has measured roughly 70–200 ms per image on a GPU and 0.5–1 second on a CPU; actual speed varies by device and image. Hikaku checks media near the viewport and caches results. You can turn the visual model off in settings and use metadata checks alone.
 What does the second opinion do? It runs another detection model and shows its result alongside the first. The model downloads once when you request it (23 MB). The models can disagree; the second model does not cancel a flag from the first.
 What do Balanced and Strict mean? Balanced flags explicit AI declarations in metadata and high-scoring visual-model estimates. Strict only flags explicit metadata declarations. Strict does not verify signatures or guarantee that a declaration is true.
 How can I report a mistake? Open the image details, use Copy JSON, and submit a detection report
. Review the copied result before posting it publicly: it can contain image URLs and metadata. Reports of false flags and missed images both help.
