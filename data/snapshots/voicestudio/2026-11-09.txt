 Quick answers
 Little questions, short answers.
 Is it really free?
 The desktop app is open source under AGPL-3.0, so yes. Pro and Lifetime are a separate build with a paid Commercial License and extra features, for teams that need commercial terms.
 Licence guide 
 Does it phone home?
 The free app needs no account or cloud, and goes online when you ask it to: checking for updates or downloading a model. Pro needs an account and sends licence and usage data.
 Privacy 
 Do I need a fancy GPU?
 Nope. About 8 GB of RAM is enough to start. A GPU just makes everything faster.
