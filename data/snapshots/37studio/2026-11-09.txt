 FAQ
 Questions people ask
 Is 37Studio free? Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT License: no trial, no license key and no paid tier. AI usage is separate and billed to your own Claude plan or Anthropic, Google and ElevenLabs accounts. If you'd rather have someone set it up for you, message us
.
 Can I contribute? Please do. The code is on GitHub
: report bugs and ask for features in Issues, and send pull requests. The README explains how to run it from source.
 Can I control how the AI replies? Yes, per brand. Choose off, drafts for approval or replies on their own; write rules for tone, sign-off, what never to promise and when to hand over to a person; and keep the knowledge base it answers from up to date. Facts only ever come from that knowledge base.
 Do I need a server? No. Everything runs on your own Windows PC, Mac or Linux machine, inside Docker or, on Windows and Linux, as the desktop app
. If you want replies and Autopilot running around the clock, you can also run it on your own Linux server with HTTPS and a password; the cloud guide
 shows how. The only things that leave your computer are calls to the AI providers and the social platforms you connect.
 What happens when my computer is off? Nothing is lost. Messenger and Instagram keep messages on Meta, and Meta retries WhatsApp messages for a while (a relay of your own keeps them 7 days). When 37Studio starts again it answers everything the platform still allows a reply to: messages from the last 24 hours and comments up to two days old. For round-the-clock replies, keep it on a computer that doesn't sleep, such as a Mac mini, or run it on a server.
 Can I use my Claude subscription instead of an API key? Yes. Run claude setup-token in Claude Code on your computer and paste the token in Settings → AI services; the agent then works within your Pro or Max plan. Anthropic's terms govern using a subscription in other apps, so an Anthropic API key is the fully supported route.
 Can I use my Runway, Kling or other video subscription? Not directly. Monthly plans for video tools such as Runway, Kling or Google AI Pro work only on their own websites; using a model from another app needs a separate API key that's billed on its own. Make the clip on their site, then upload it to a project or paste a Google Drive link, and the agent edits it like any other footage. For clips generated inside 37Studio, add a Gemini key. More video models are on our list.
 When does the agent generate images, clips or voice? Only when the footage and the brand's library can't do the job, because every generation is billed. It tells you what it generated, and the cost shows on the project. On Autopilot it decides on its own. The budget in Settings caps Claude's spend per turn; generated media is billed on top, so keep an eye on the Costs page.
 Does it only make videos? No. It also makes thumbnails, square and portrait posts, announcements and covers in each brand's look, and can generate images with Gemini. Videos post straight to every platform you connect; images download as PNG for now.
 Which platforms can it post to? Facebook Pages, Instagram business accounts, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest boards, Bluesky and Mastodon, many accounts per brand. Bluesky and Mastodon connect with an app password or token. The others need a free developer app of your own, set up once by following the setup guide
, including a Meta app for Facebook and Instagram. Until each platform reviews your app, YouTube keeps uploads private and TikTok allows only private posts or drafts. Threads only accepts videos from a public web address, which an app on your computer doesn't have; Meta removed posting to Groups and personal profiles; and Reddit and Snapchat have no usable API for posting videos.
 Does WhatsApp work? Yes, in beta, for a number on the WhatsApp Business API. Meta delivers WhatsApp messages only to a public web address, so you give 37Studio one with a free Cloudflare Tunnel that opens only the webhook path; the connect guide
 walks through it. Keeping the same number on the WhatsApp Business phone app at the same time needs Meta to approve your app as a Tech Provider, so use a number dedicated to the API.
 Why is the first download so big? The Docker version is about 3 GB, once. It bundles every tool its AI agent works with, so you install nothing else: Claude Code and OpenCode, the HyperFrames video engine with its own Chrome for rendering, ffmpeg for cutting and encoding, Node.js, Python, and fonts for Bangla, English and emoji. Updates after that only download what changed. The desktop app
 is a much smaller download, because it uses the tools you install on your computer instead.
 Which languages does it work in? Any language Claude writes, from English and Spanish to Hindi and Bangla. Videos, voiceovers, captions and replies follow the language you choose, and replies follow the customer's language and script. Long recordings, like a 2-hour class, are transcribed on your own computer with an open Whisper model that knows about 100 languages: no per-minute fees, and the audio never leaves your PC.
 Where is my data? Do you collect anything? Everything lives in a data folder next to the app on your computer (or your server): brands, footage, finished videos, messages and settings, with your keys encrypted. We don't collect it, and the app sends nothing to us. The privacy page
 lists everything it sends anywhere, such as the update check to GitHub.
