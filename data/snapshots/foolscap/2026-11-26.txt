HOW IT WORKS
Bring in your draft, or write in it. Word, Scrivener, Google Docs (as .docx), Markdown, a whole Obsidian vault.
Foolscap reads it. Facts that don't agree between chapters, threads you raise and never settle, passages a reader may stumble on, who is who, each pointed at your own words.
You decide. Take a point up, set it aside, or call it deliberate. Foolscap never writes or rewrites a word of your book.
YOUR BOOK STAYS YOURS
Never used to train any AI, by us or by anyone we work with.
Nobody at Foolscap reads it. It is encrypted where it is kept.
Make a book private, and nothing of it is sent to any AI.
Take all of it with you, or delete it, whenever you like.
