 Pricing questions, answered
 Everything you need to know about plans, billing, and what&#x27;s included.
 Can I start free?
 Yes. Free includes 10,000 characters per month, one connected source, 25 active relationships, 25 Engage actions, three person research runs, a daily briefing, 40+ writing use cases, and 30+ languages.
 What&#x27;s included in Lite?
 Lite includes everything in Free and removes the monthly character limit.
 What&#x27;s included in Personal?
 Personal adds two connected sources, 250 active relationships, 250 Engage actions per month, and 10 person research runs per month.
 What&#x27;s included in Pro?
 Pro adds all connected sources, 1,000 active relationships, 2,000 Engage actions per month, 50 person research runs per month, morning and evening briefings, and custom use cases.
 How does yearly billing work?
 Yearly billing saves 20% compared with the monthly price and is billed annually.
 Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?
 Yes, you can change plans at any time.
 Can I cancel?
 Yes, cancel anytime. You keep access until the end of your billing period.
