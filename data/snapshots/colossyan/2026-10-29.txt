 Frequently asked questions
 How much does Colossyan cost?
 Our free plan gives you $27/month worth of value, including our entire 300+ avatar library
 with best-in-class avatars. When you need more, Professional is $59/month on an annual plan, giving you 30 minutes a month with NEO and 10 minutes a month with NEO2 generation
.
 Can I try Colossyan for free before signing up?
 Yes. Colossyan has a generous free trial and a free plan, so you can explore the product and create your first AI video before you commit. Start a free account
, or book a personal demo
 with our sales team to learn more.
 What's the difference between Starter, Professional, and Enterprise?
 Starter is free for individuals exploring AI video. Professional ($59/month annual) adds NEO2 minutes, SCORM export, AI image generation, and up to 3 editors for small teams. Enterprise unlocks unlimited minutes, unlimited SCORM, SSO, data residency, and a dedicated CSM. See Enterprise plans
.
 Is Colossyan SOC 2 Type II compliant and GDPR ready?
 Yes. Colossyan is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant, with EU or US data residency on Enterprise plans. Enterprise customers can request a full security packet including the SOC 2 report, penetration test summary, and DPA. See our security page
.
 Does Colossyan use my videos to train AI models?
 No. Colossyan does not train AI models on customer content. Your scripts, recordings, and generated videos stay private to your workspace. Data processing terms are covered in the DPA, available for Enterprise plans
.
 Does Colossyan support SCORM export and LMS integration?
 Yes. Professional includes 5 SCORM exports per month. Enterprise includes unlimited SCORM in streaming and self-contained formats, compatible with any SCORM 1.2 or 2004 LMS (Workday, Cornerstone, Docebo, Litmos, Absorb). Learn more about SCORM export and LMS integration
.
 What languages does Colossyan support?
 Colossyan supports 120+ languages. Avatar narration, on-screen text, and interactive elements localize together in a single workflow, with lip-sync in every language. Explore our full avatar library
.
 How much does a Colossyan custom avatar cost?
 Instant custom avatars are included in any active plan. However, studio custom avatars
, which are filmed professionally using a green screen, are available as an add-on for $1000 per year.
 Can I purchase additional minutes?
 Yes. On a Professional yearly plan you can buy add-on minute packs: 600 extra NEO2 minutes for $1,500/year. Reach out to sales
 to add a pack.
 Do you offer migration support from another platform?
 Yes. Enterprise plans include a dedicated onboarding specialist who audits content from Synthesia, HeyGen, Articulate, or Camtasia and plans the migration. Most teams are productive within their first week. See how Colossyan compares to other platforms
.
 Will my unused minutes roll over to the next month?
 No. If you are under your video minute limit at the end of a billing period, your unused minutes will not roll over.
 If I pay for an annual subscription, will I receive my entire minute allowance for the year all at once?
 Yes! You'll receive all of your minutes for the year upon purchasing an annual plan, giving you the flexibility to use them whenever you need them.
 What counts toward my minute allowance?
 Only generated videos count, based on their length. Drafts do not. Videos with a NEO2 avatar use your NEO2 minutes. Videos with a NEO avatar, and videos with no avatar at all, use your NEO minutes.
 Do my drafts count toward my minute allowance?
 No. Only generated videos count toward your allowance, so feel free to create as many drafts as you like!
 If I delete my generated videos, do I get my minutes back toward my allowance?
 No, deleting generated videos will not result in a credit of minutes to your allowance.
 Can I pause my Colossyan subscription?
 Yes, you can pause your account for up to 2 months for free. Reach out to us in the chat to do so.
 Can I cancel Colossyan at any time?
 Monthly plans can be canceled at any time. Annual plans can also be canceled, but since they are paid upfront for the year, your subscription stays active and fully accessible until the end of your current billing period. Unused months are not refunded.
 Is Colossyan a one-time payment?
 No, access to Colossyan requires a recurring subscription. Professional plans can be billed monthly or annually, and all Enterprise plans
 are billed annually.
