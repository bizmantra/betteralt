 FAQs
 Can’t find the answer here? Contact support
 How does Dovetail One pricing work?
 Dovetail One pricing has three parts: the platform, the users who can access your workspace, and credits, a shared AI usage balance for data processing and AI features. All three are billed together, annually. Credits are 15c each on every plan. Bronze is $4,800 a year for the platform and 5 users, paid by card. For Silver and Gold, talk to sales.
 What uses credits?
 Processing a data point uses 1 credit. That covers everything Dovetail does to it, from transcription and translation to classification, CRM enrichment, and indexing for semantic search. Chat responses, Digital Twin responses, and agent runs use credits based on the work needed to complete the task.
 How are redaction credits counted?
 Redaction uses 1 credit per data point, on top of the 1 credit used to process it. A transcript or recording is one data point, so redacting it uses 1 credit no matter how many redactions it has or how long the recording is. Suggested redactions you don’t apply don’t use credits.
 Do actions that fail or are canceled use credits?
 No. Credits are only used when an action finishes successfully. If a recording can’t be transcribed, an import is canceled, or you stop a Chat response before it’s complete, it doesn’t use credits. If Dovetail tries again after an error, the action only uses credits once.
 Does Dovetail in Slack and Teams use credits?
 Yes. Chat responses in Slack and Teams use credits from your workspace’s balance, including answers to questions from people who aren’t Dovetail users.
 What happens when we use all our contracted credits?
 Credit overages are on by default, so your workspace keeps working and additional credits are billed monthly, with no overage penalty. Workspace admins can turn overages off in Settings, and then data processing, Chat, agents, and Digital Twins stop until more credits are available.
 Do unused credits roll over?
 No. Credits are valid for the length of your contract, usually one year. Unused credits expire at the end of the contract and don’t roll over at renewal.
 Can we set credit budgets per user or feature?
 No. Credits are a single balance shared by the whole workspace. Every user can use Chat, agents, and Channels, plus Digital Twins on Silver and Gold, and all usage draws from the same balance.
 Can we change our users or credits during the contract?
 Bronze comes with a fixed number of users (5). On Silver and Gold, add users in the app whenever you need them, and they’re available immediately. Silver and Gold can also turn on user overages. On every plan, you can top up credits in the app at any time. You can’t reduce users or credits below your contracted amount until renewal. Admins count toward your number of users.
