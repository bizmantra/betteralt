 FAQs
 Can’t find the answer here? Contact support
 How many users do I need?
 Users contribute data to—and do analysis within—your customer intelligence platform. Most customers buy enough users for their product management, design, customer experience, strategy, and innovation teams. Some also buy users for their sales, customer success, support, and marketing teams who also contribute data to and leverage on customer feedback.
 How do we control who sees what in Dovetail?
 Access is controlled by changing a user’s role—Manager, Contributor, or Viewer—or by restricting a project or a folder to a specific user. Admins can set up user groups to make this easier on our Enterprise plans.
 Can we pause our subscription?
 There’s no way to pause a subscription today, but you’re not locked into paying if your team needs a break—downgrading to the Free plan is available any time, and you can move back to a paid plan whenever you’re ready.
 What happens if we cancel our subscription?
 You’ll keep full access through the end of the billing period you’ve already paid for, so there’s no refund for that remaining time since you’re still using the product during it. Once the period ends, your workspace moves into a 30-day read-only window so you can export your data—reach out to support if you need a hand—and after that, it’s deleted in line with your data retention policy.
 Can you send me an invoice?
 Yes—Enterprise plans are invoiced with standard net-30 terms, so your finance team can process it as a normal vendor invoice rather than needing a credit card on file.
 Can we pay in other currencies?
 Dovetail bills in USD across every plan, which keeps pricing consistent no matter where your team is based. If a USD-denominated card is the obstacle rather than the currency itself, Enterprise plans can be invoiced instead of charged to a card, with payment still processed in USD.
 How is tax calculated?
 All prices are tax exclusive, and additional taxes (GST, VAT, US Sales Tax, etc.) may be applied to your invoice.
