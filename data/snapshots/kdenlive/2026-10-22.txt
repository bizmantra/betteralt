 Free and Open Source Video Editor
 Kdenlive is the acronym for KDE Non-Linear Video Editor. It works on Linux, Windows, macOS, and BSD.

 Powerful Tools from Start to Finish
 Whether you're doing simple edits or crafting a professional masterpiece, Kdenlive supports you from ingest to the final touch, with a customizable interface
 that adapts to your workflow.
 Asset Management
 Ingest footage in almost any audio or video format
, review its metadata
 or use proxies
 for smooth playback even on low-end systems.
 Use powerful tags and filters
 to organize your assets and create multiple bins
, so everything is at hand when you need it.
 Editing
 Take control of your edit with a flexible non-linear workflow, multicam
 and 3-point editing
. Manage complex timelines with nested sequences
 and markers
 while automatic backups
 keep your work protected.
 Audio
 Can you hear the difference? Shape your soundtrack with powerful mixers
, built-in voiceover recording
, and an audio spectrum
 that lets you see exactly what you’re hearing.
 Effects and Transitions
 Choose from hundreds of audio
 and video
 effects, apply them at any level
 of your workflow and create dynamic changes with keyframes
.
 Add smooth transitions
 between clips and combine layers with flexible compositions
 to create polished, dynamic sequences.
 Color
 Bring your footage to life! Apply LUTs
, do basic color corrections or detailed creative grading
.
 Analyze your image with histogram
, vectorscope
, RGB parade and waveform
 to make precise adjustments.
 Subtitles and Text
 Create professional subtitles
 with automatic transcription
, advanced layering and styling.
 Add simple title clips
 with ease or seamlessly connect your project to Glaxnimate
 for complex motion graphics.
 Wait, there is more!
