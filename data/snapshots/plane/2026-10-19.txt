What can we answer for you today?
What is Plane?
Plane is an open-source project management tool that helps teams collaborate, track progress, and deliver projects efficiently. It combines project management, wiki, and intake forms in one unified platform.
Is Plane free to use?
Yes, Plane offers a generous free tier with core features. We also have paid plans for advanced features, enterprise needs, and priority support.
Can I self-host Plane?
Absolutely! Plane is open-source and can be self-hosted on your own infrastructure for complete control and privacy. We provide comprehensive documentation and support for self-hosting.
What integrations does Plane support?
Plane integrates with popular tools like GitHub, GitLab, Slack, Discord, and many more to streamline your workflow and keep your team connected.
How do I migrate from other project management tools?
We provide migration tools and support to help you move from tools like Jira, Asana, Trello, or Linear to Plane. Our team can assist with the migration process.
Is my data secure?
Security is our top priority. We use industry-standard encryption, regular security audits, and follow best practices to protect your data. For self-hosted instances, you have complete control over your data.
