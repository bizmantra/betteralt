 Questions. 
 Pricing, answered.
 Something else? Email team@warmbly.com
.
 Do you charge per email sent? 
 No. Each plan includes a daily send budget. Unused volume doesn’t carry over to the next day.
 Is warmup charged separately? 
 No. Warmup is unlimited on every paid plan and runs in the premium pool.
 Is there really no mailbox limit? 
 Mailboxes are unlimited on every paid plan under fair use: one mailbox for every email a day your plan includes, so Grow holds 3,000 and Business 15,000. If you need more, ask from the connect dialog and we usually answer within a business day.
 Is there a free trial? 
 Yes. A new workspace gets 14 days free with no card: two mailboxes warming in the free pool, 20 cold emails a day, the unified inbox and campaigns. Each owner gets one trial, on their first workspace. After it, connecting and warming mailboxes needs a paid plan, from $29 a month, and nothing you set up is deleted. Workspaces that already had the free plan keep it.
 Can I pay for warmup only? 
 Not any more. The Warmup plan is no longer sold, and workspaces already on it keep it while they stay subscribed. Every paid plan includes unlimited warmup in the premium pool.
 What does self-hosting cost? 
 Nothing. Warmbly is Apache 2.0, free to run on your own servers and fully functional on its own. Its warmup only pairs the mailboxes on your own instance, with no shared pool, and self-hosted instances do not link to the Warmbly Cloud pool. If deliverability is what you need, Warmbly Cloud is the better choice. Self-hosting suits experienced operators who want to own the whole stack: Warmbly does not support self-hosted instances, and help comes from the community on Discord.
 Is there a charge per seat? 
 No. Add as many teammates as you like. Roles and the audit log come with Business.
 Do you charge per client for agencies? 
 No. An agency plan covers every client workspace, with no limit on clients. Clients share one monthly send pool instead of a daily cap each, and you can add 50,000-send top-up packs when it runs low.
 Can I pay yearly? 
 Yes, with 20% off. Use the switch at the top of this page.
 Can I buy as a business? 
 Yes. Add your legal name and tax ID at checkout. They appear on your invoices, and reverse charge is applied where the rules allow it. Taxes are calculated from your billing address and shown before you pay.
 What is your refund policy? 
 A full refund within 14 days of a paid purchase if Warmbly didn’t work for you.
 Do you have startup pricing? 
 50% off Grow for a year for pre-seed and seed companies from YC, Antler, EF, Techstars and similar programmes.
