 Questions. 
 Pricing, answered.
 Something else? Email team@warmbly.com
.
 Do you charge per email sent? 
 No. Each plan includes a daily send budget. Unused volume doesn’t carry over to the next day.
 Is warmup charged separately? 
 No. Warmup is unlimited on every paid plan and runs in the premium pool.
 Is there really no mailbox limit? 
 Mailboxes are unlimited on every paid plan under fair use: one mailbox for every email a day your plan includes, so Grow holds 3,000 and Business 15,000. If you need more, ask from the connect dialog and we usually answer within a business day.
 Is there a free plan? 
 Yes. A hosted workspace starts free with up to 10 mailboxes warming, no card and no time limit. Sending, the inbox and the CRM unlock when you pick a plan.
 Can I pay for warmup only? 
 Not any more. The Warmup plan is no longer sold, and workspaces already on it keep it while they stay subscribed. Warm up to 10 mailboxes for free, or pick any paid plan for unlimited warmup in the premium pool.
 What does self-hosting cost? 
 Nothing. Warmbly is Apache 2.0, free to run on your own servers and fully functional on its own. Its warmup only pairs your own mailboxes, which is not effective; the shared pool is on Warmbly Cloud once it launches, and self-hosted instances do not link to it. Self-hosting suits experienced operators: Warmbly does not support self-hosted instances, and help comes from the community on Discord.
 Is there a charge per seat? 
 No. Add as many teammates as you like. Roles and the audit log come with Business.
 Can I pay yearly? 
 Yes, with 20% off. Use the switch at the top of this page.
 Can I buy as a business? 
 Yes. Add your legal name and tax ID at checkout. They appear on your invoices, and reverse charge is applied where the rules allow it. Taxes are calculated from your billing address and shown before you pay.
 What is your refund policy? 
 A full refund within 14 days of a paid purchase if Warmbly didn’t work for you.
 Do you have startup pricing? 
 50% off Grow for a year for pre-seed and seed companies from YC, Antler, EF, Techstars and similar programmes.
