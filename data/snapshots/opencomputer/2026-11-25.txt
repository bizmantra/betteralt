Self-hosting
 Turn any VM into a multiplayer coding agent.
 Self-hosted, it runs on one VM per organization in your Azure, AWS or Google Cloud account. The VM connects out to the gateway and needs no open inbound ports.
 Many sessions on one VM.
 Each thread gets its own session and its own copy of your repositories. One VM can often run hundreds at once.
 Disk first, then git.
 Sessions write state to the VM's disk, then persist it to a git store. Another VM can pick up the same session, so you scale out by adding VMs.
 Sandboxes only for heavy work.
 A session moves to a sandbox for a heavy build or a preview environment, not on every turn.

 Plans cover the whole workspace. Past your plan's minutes, each extra minute is $0.01, paid from prepaid credits: packs of $10, $50, $200, with optional auto top-up. Nothing is billed after the fact. Models are yours at no charge from us, or ours at API prices with no markup, also from credits.
 What's a work minute?
 The clock runs while it works
 From the moment @
Computer picks up your message to its reply: reading code, running commands, builds and tests. Metered by the second.
 Waiting is free
 Time between messages, idle threads and time spent waiting on you to review costs nothing. Two threads working at once count as two. Your serverless agents draw on the same minutes.
 Why minutes go further
 Workspaces resume warm. Your repo, dependencies and build caches are already there, so a minute goes on your work, not on cloning and reinstalling.
