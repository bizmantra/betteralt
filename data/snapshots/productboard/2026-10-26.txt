 Frequently Asked Questions
 What is Productboard? Productboard is the AI-powered product management platform that helps product teams turn customer signals into clear priorities, align stakeholders around what&#x27;s being built, and move from strategy to delivery - faster. Every plan includes Spark, our AI built for PMs, which brings context-aware intelligence to your entire workflow: from analyzing customer feedback to drafting product specs and planning roadmaps.
 What&#x27;s the difference between a maker and contributor? Makers are the product managers and leads who own the product strategy — they create roadmaps, prioritize features, and manage insights. Pricing is based on the number of makers in your workspace.
 Contributors are colleagues who submit feedback, tag insights, and collaborate via documents and comments — great for engineers, designers, or customer success teams. They don&#x27;t count toward your maker limit.
 Which plan is right for my team? Business is designed for teams that move together with AI, sharing context, roadmaps and signal for improved results.
 Enterprise is designed for organizations that need security, governance, and rollout support at scale.
 Not sure? Talk to our team and we’ll help you find the right fit.
 Can I switch plans or add makers later? Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or add makers at any time. If you upgrade mid-cycle, costs and credits are pro-rated for the remainder of your billing period.
 What is a credit, and how many do I get? Credits power all of Spark&#x27;s AI capabilities — from analyzing customer feedback and identifying trends to generating documents and competitive analyses. Different tasks use different amounts depending on their complexity: analyzing 100 customer feedback items uses around 30-50 credits, while creating a comprehensive specification might use 50-200, for example.
 Business includes 500 credits per maker each month. Enterprise includes 1,500 base credits plus 800 per additional maker each month.
 What happens when I run out of credits? You keep full access to your workspace, documents, and feedback - nothing gets locked or deleted. You just won&#x27;t be able to use Spark&#x27;s AI features until your credits reset at your next monthly renewal, or until you purchase a top-up. You&#x27;ll see notifications in Productboard as you approach your limit.
 Can I buy more credits? Yes. Top-ups are available directly from your workspace: 50-credit bundles for $5 on monthly plans, or 600-credit bundles for $60/year on annual plans. You can buy as many as you need, and purchases mid-cycle are pro-rated.
 Can I add Spark to my existing Productboard workspace? Spark is currently available as standard for all new Business and Enterprise Productboard workspaces.
 We&#x27;re also busy rolling Spark out to all our existing customers. If you&#x27;re interested, get in touch with us at support@productboard.com
 or through your account representative.
 What data does Spark use, and how is it protected? Spark works with documents, customer feedback, and associated data you provide in your workspace. Your data stays within your workspace and is never used to train external AI models. Spark follows Productboard&#x27;s standard security and privacy practices - see our AI Terms
 for full details.
 Have additional questions? Send us an email to  hello@productboard.com
