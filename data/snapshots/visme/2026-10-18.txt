 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
 Can Visme be free?
 Yes. Visme offers a free plan that lets you create presentations, infographics, documents and other visual content using a limited set of templates, assets and AI tools. Paid plans unlock advanced features like brand governance, analytics, premium templates and team collaboration.
 What does Visme cost?
 Visme offers a free plan, with paid plans starting at $12.25 per month for individuals and $24.75 per month for teams when billed annually. Enterprise pricing is available for organizations that need advanced security, governance and onboarding support.
 How do you cancel a Visme subscription?
 To cancel a Visme subscription, log into your account, go to Account Settings → Billing, and select Cancel Subscription. Your plan will remain active until the end of the current billing period, after which it will revert to the free plan.
 Is Visme worth the money?
 Visme is worth the cost for teams that need to create on-brand presentations, reports, infographics and interactive content at scale. It combines design tools, brand governance, analytics and collaboration features that many businesses use to manage visual communication across teams.
 Do I have to pay to use Visme?
 Our Basic plan is free, so you can try Visme, and no credit card information is required to sign up. You only choose to upgrade your account if you need access to premium features such as download options.
 How do paid plans work?
 The payments are in US dollars and made automatically for your convenience. You can cancel anytime and you’re not going to be charged for the upcoming cycle.
 What's included in Visme's free plan?
 Visme's free plan includes templates, the drag-and-drop editor, basic charts, and storage for your projects. Paid plans add premium templates, brand kits, analytics, and more download formats.
 How do I upgrade my Free account to Paid?
 Simply create a free account and from your dashboard area click on "Upgrade to Premium." Pick one of our paid plans according to your needs, enter payment information, and enjoy the premium features!
 Can I make some or all of my content private?
 Yes. In Business plans you have the option to make some or all of your content private. In business and enterprise plans you can also share projects to be editable among one or more team members of your choosing.
 We're interested in Team/Enterprise account. How can we set up a live demo?
 We provide live demos for Team accounts of 5 or more users. Please contact us via [email protected]
 and one of our product specialists will get back to you to set up a demo and show you how you can transform the way you create and share content at your organization.
 What happens to my content when I cancel my subscription?
 When you cancel your subscription, your account will be converted to a free account with limited features; however, you can still access the content that you have previously created. Please note that Visme branding will appear on the material you’ve published online. You can always upgrade your account again to access premium features.
 Can I get an educational or non-profit discount?
 We have special plans for education users and the discounts are already included. For non-profit discounts, please contact us via [email protected]
 and we will get back to you within 1 business day with the non-profit discount.
 How do I sign up for an education plan?
 You need to sign up with your ".edu" email. If you’re associated with an academic institution but don’t own an ".edu" email, please contact us via [email protected]
 for further assistance in signing up. Education plans are available and paid annually. The payments are in US dollars and made automatically every 12 months for your convenience. You can cancel at any time before your next billing period and you’re not going to be charged for the upcoming cycle.
 Is there any limit on how often I can download or share my content on Visme?
 No! You can share and download your content as often as you like under any of the premium plans.
