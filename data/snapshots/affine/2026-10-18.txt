 Frequently asked questions
 What is AFFiNE?
 AFFiNE is the open-source, local-first knowledge workspace that combines documents, whiteboards, and AI in one canvas. AFFiNE Cloud is hosted by our team; the editor and most of the codebase are MIT-licensed, while the self-hosted backend is source-available under the AFFiNE Enterprise Edition license. (Not to be confused with affine geometry in mathematics.)
 Is AFFiNE free?
 Yes. AFFiNE Cloud Free includes unlimited local workspaces, 10 GB of Cloud Storage, real-time collaboration with up to 3 members per workspace, and 7 days of version history. There is no time limit on the Free plan. For self-hosting, the editor and most of the codebase are MIT-licensed, while the backend is source-available under the AFFiNE Enterprise Edition license: without a commercial agreement, you may review the backend source code only. Modifying, compiling, or redistributing the EE-licensed backend code requires commercial authorization from AFFiNE.
 What is the difference between AFFiNE Cloud and Self-Hosted?
 AFFiNE Cloud runs on our infrastructure with no setup. Storage, seats, and version-history limits depend on your plan (Free, Pro, or Team). Self-Hosted
 runs the full stack on your own servers; the editor is MIT, the backend uses the AFFiNE Enterprise Edition license. You control all data, and limits are bound by your own infrastructure. The Self-Hosted Team tier ($10/seat/month) adds priority support, multi-admin roles, and scaling for 10+ seats.
 Can I use AFFiNE as a free Notion alternative?
 Yes. AFFiNE's editor and most of the codebase are open-source under MIT, local-first by design, and free for personal use. As an open-source Notion alternative
, your workspace data lives on your device with optional cloud sync. The EE-licensed backend source is available for review only without a commercial agreement; modifying, compiling, or redistributing it requires commercial authorization from AFFiNE.
 What is included in the AFFiNE Free plan?
 Local side: unlimited local workspaces, unlimited customization, unlimited Doc and Edgeless editing. Cloud Basic side: 10 GB of Cloud Storage, 10 MB maximum file size, up to 3 members per Workspace, 7 days of Cloud Time Machine version history, and up to 3 login devices.
 What is the AFFiNE Believer Plan?
 The Believer Plan is a one-time purchase of $499.99 that gives you lifetime personal usage of AFFiNE Pro, including everything in Pro plus 1 TB of Cloud Storage. It is a single payment with no subscription, designed for early supporters who want to back the project long-term.
 Can I self-host AFFiNE for free?
 AFFiNE uses a dual-license model: the editor ( BlockSuite
), desktop app, and most of the codebase are MIT-licensed, while the backend server ( packages/backend ) uses the AFFiNE Enterprise Edition (EE) license. Without a commercial agreement, you may review the backend source code only. Modifying, compiling, or redistributing the EE-licensed backend code requires commercial authorization from AFFiNE.

 Can AFFiNE replace Confluence for self-hosted team knowledge bases?
 Yes. For teams moving off Confluence's cloud per-seat cost, AFFiNE's Self-Hosted Team tier ($10/seat/month, 10+ seats) is built as a team knowledge base
 that runs the full stack on your own infrastructure with priority support, multi-admin roles, unlimited team members, and full data residency. Unlike Confluence's wiki-only model, AFFiNE combines docs, whiteboards, and AI in one canvas. It works as a flexible corporate wiki
 with local-first data ownership and an open-source editor under MIT.
