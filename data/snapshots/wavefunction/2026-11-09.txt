 Frequently asked questions
 Is Wavefunction free? Yes. Wavefunction is free and open source under the MIT license. You bring your own models or provider account; any model usage charges come from your provider.
 Which models can I use? Sign in with ChatGPT, use an API key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter or Vercel AI Gateway, or run models locally through Ollama, LM Studio or another OpenAI-compatible server. Choose the model that fits each conversation.
 How do I keep my work private? Your conversations and settings are stored on your computer. Use a local model to keep model inference local, or configure a provider with zero data retention. The models and connectors you choose determine which services receive your data.
 How does parallel work happen? Describe what you want in one conversation. Wavefunction can split work across subagents, take follow-up instructions while they run, and bring their work back together. You can keep talking as the work moves forward.
 Can I use my existing tools and instructions? Yes. Connect your tools through the built-in connector catalogue or add an MCP server. Wavefunction also reads your AGENTS.md instructions and loads SKILL.md skills, so it can work with the setup you already use.
 Which platforms are supported? Wavefunction is currently available for macOS. Download the app, connect a model, and start a conversation.
