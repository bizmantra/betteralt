Highlights
 Word processing
 What&#x27;s inside
.
 01
 The ribbon you know
 Tabs, groups, shortcuts and the live Styles gallery. Enter continues a list, Tab demotes it, and formatting works as your hands expect.
 02
 Real document formats
 Opens and saves .docx natively, imports and exports .odt, .rtf, .html, .md and .txt, and exports clean PDF with selectable text.
 03
 Instant layout
 Paragraph layout is cached: typing in a 188-page document re-lays it out in about 1.4 ms, with pages rendered on demand.
 04
 Track changes & review
 Comment balloons in the margin, accept and reject changes, document comparison, plus offline spelling and grammar as you type.
 05
 Academic & business references
 Generate tables of contents, footnotes, citations and bibliographies in APA, MLA, Chicago and IEEE styles, indexes, and captions.
 06
 Agent-ready commands
 389 discrete commands drive the ribbon, keyboard shortcuts, a command-line tool, a JSON control channel and an MCP server.
