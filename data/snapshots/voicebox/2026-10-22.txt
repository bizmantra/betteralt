 Questions
 Is the app really free?
 Yes — Voicebox is free and open source, forever. Cloning, dictation, every TTS engine, MCP, personalities: all of it runs locally with no account. The paid plans only add optional cloud backup & sync.
 What&#x27;s encrypted in the cloud?
 Everything. Your profiles, generations, and captures are end-to-end encrypted on your device before upload. The server stores only ciphertext and can never read your data.
 What counts toward storage?
 Your encrypted objects — generated audio, the original audio kept with each capture, and profile data. Plans differ mainly on storage, device count, and version-history length.
 Can I cancel anytime?
 Yes. Cloud is a subscription you can cancel whenever you like; your local library always stays on your machine and keeps working.
 Cloud pricing and limits are not final — they&#x27;ll be confirmed at launch.
