 Frequently asked questions about pricing
 How can I try HeyGen?
 You can try HeyGen for free, with no credit card required. The Free plan includes up to 3 videos per month and limited trial access to premium features such as Avatar 6, Video Agent and lip-sync translation, so you can explore the product before upgrading.
 How much does HeyGen cost?
 You can use HeyGen for free and generate up to 3 videos a month, with no credit card required. Paid plans include a monthly credit allocation that you can use across all features. For individual creators, the Creator plan costs $29/month (or $24/month when billed annually). For individuals with more advanced needs, Pro starts at $49/month, with higher tiers offering more monthly credits. For teams that want to create and collaborate, the Business plan costs $149/month plus $20/seat/month.
 What are credits?
 Credits are HeyGen&#x27;s single currency for creating videos and assets. Every paid plan includes a monthly allocation of credits that you can use across HeyGen features. The number of credits required for a video or asset can vary depending on the AI model used, the video duration and the complexity of the generation.
 Are unused credits carried forward?
 Yes. Monthly subscribers’ unused credits carry forward for one additional month. Annual subscribers’ credits accumulate until their annual renewal date. Credits do not carry forward after cancellation — credits from your previous billing cycle will expire if your subscription is no longer active.
 How can I get more credits?
 Creator and Pro users can upgrade to a higher plan or tier for additional credits. Business users can purchase one-time credit packs or enable auto-reload. Upgrades take effect immediately, with billing adjusted on a pro-rata basis and credits added to your account straight away.
 How do the Pro plan tiers differ?
 All Pro tiers offer the same features; only the monthly credit allocation varies. Tiers range from 1,000 credits per month at $49 to 100,000 credits per month at $4,300. You can switch tiers at any time.
 What is the difference between a monthly and an annual subscription?
 Monthly subscriptions are billed every month; annual subscriptions are billed once a year at a lower effective monthly rate. Annual subscribers also benefit from a longer credit carry-forward period: credits accumulate throughout the year until renewal, rather than for just one billing cycle.
 Can I switch or cancel my plan at any time?
 Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately, with pro-rata billing and credits added straight away. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing cycle, so you retain your plan and credits until then. For support, contact us at [email protected]
.
 What happens when I cancel my subscription?
 Cancelling stops your subscription from renewing at the next billing date. You retain access to your paid features and remaining credits until the end of your current billing cycle, after which your account moves to the Free plan. Unused credits do not carry forward.
