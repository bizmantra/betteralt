 Frequently asked questions
 Should I upgrade to Pro or Premium?
 If you share lots of videos with users, customers, or followers, Premium is the better fit. Otherwise, Pro should be enough. You can change plans at any time.
 How do Tella Agent credits work?
 Credits pay for the Tella Agent, the AI editor that edits your video from a chat in the editor. Each message uses some credits, depending on how much work the agent does and which model it uses. Pro includes 3,000 credits per user each month and Premium 6,000, pooled across the workspace. Included credits reset monthly and don&#x27;t roll over. Enterprise credits depend on your contract. Free trials don&#x27;t include credits. How credits work
.
 Can I buy more credits?
 Yes. Workspace owners can buy one-time top-ups: 10,000 credits for $10, 50,000 for $45 or 100,000 for $80. Purchased credits never expire and are only used once the month&#x27;s included credits run out.
 Do you offer SSO and/or SCIM?
 Yes. SSO and SCIM are available as add-ons on any paid plan and are included with Enterprise. Learn more on our Enterprise page
.
 Is Tella SOC 2 compliant?
 Yes. Tella is SOC 2 Type II compliant on every plan. The SOC 2 Type II report is available to Enterprise customers under NDA. Learn more in the Tella Trust Center
.
 Do you offer any education discounts?
 Yes. See our Tella for Education
 program.
 Do you offer any nonprofit discounts?
 Yes. See our Tella for Good
 program.
 Can I cancel at any time?
 Yes. Your subscription stops at the end of the current billing period, and you will not be charged for the next one.
