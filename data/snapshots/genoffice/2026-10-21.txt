 GenOffice / FAQ
 A few things to know.
 Is GenOffice free? The office apps are free to download and use, with no ads or watermarks. AI features use Genspark credits or your own API key; your provider’s usage charges may apply.
 Can I use my existing Office files? GenOffice opens and saves .docx, .xlsx, and .pptx files, and edits PDF, HTML, and Markdown. Complex formatting can vary; check important files before sharing them.
 How do I convert a PDF to Word? Open your PDF in GenOffice, choose Word from the Convert PDF menu, then edit the resulting .docx. Conversion runs locally for free, without AI credits. Scanned-page OCR is supported on macOS and Windows. Check tables and formatting before sharing.
 Can I edit PDF text and images directly? Yes. GenOffice supports direct text and image editing, annotations and forms. Open the file in the desktop app, make your changes and save locally. Review fonts and layout after editing.
 Does it work offline? What about privacy? Opening, editing, saving, and PDF-to-Office conversion run locally. Online AI sends requests when you use it. Official builds also send limited usage statistics by default; you can turn them off in Settings → General. See the privacy policy for details.
 Do I need a Genspark account for AI? You can sign in with Genspark or bring your own model API key. Built-in providers include Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, and more, with support for custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints.
