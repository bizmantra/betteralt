 FAQ
 Do you offer refunds? 
 We do not offer refunds but you can cancel your subscription before the next billing cycle or before your trial ends by navigating to your account page and clicking on “Manage Plan”.
 What forms of payment do you accept? 
 We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal.
 How do I contact the support team? 
 You can contact the support team through the chatbot in the lower right-hand corner of the Help Center
 or email: help@wordtune.com
 Do you offer discounts for nonprofits, NGOs or academia?
