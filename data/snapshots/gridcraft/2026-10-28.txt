Highlights
 Spreadsheets & data analysis
 What&#x27;s inside
.
 01
 The spreadsheet workflow
 Formula bar, Name Box, sheet tabs, fill handle, colored cell references, conditional formatting, and familiar shortcuts.
 02
 Native XLSX compatibility
 Styles, themes, formulas, dynamic arrays, tables, validation, comments, sparklines and PivotTables round-trip cleanly.
 03
 500+ calculation functions
 Full dependency-graph calculation engine with dynamic arrays, spilling, LET, LAMBDA, MAP, REDUCE, and table references.
 04
 Charts & PivotTables
 Seventeen chart types with customizable formatting, plus PivotTables with filters, field lists, layouts, and date grouping.
 05
 High-performance engine
 Copy-on-write workbooks with instant undo snapshots, incremental recalculation, and a virtualised grid.
 06
 Agent-native automation
 Every ribbon button, menu item, and formula can be driven by AI agents over an MCP server, CLI, or JSON control channel.
