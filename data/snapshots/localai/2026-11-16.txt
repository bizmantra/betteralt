 Files and Sandbox
 Files in. Python in a box
.
 Put a spreadsheet in Files and ask for a chart. The Sandbox runs Python on it and saves the result in Files.
 Your files stay on your server, and an agent can read and search them.
 The sandbox has no internet unless an admin allows it.
 Every change is listed in History, with an Undo button.
 Sandbox is an add-on. An admin installs it once.
 In development builds, coming in the next release.

 Home, pages and triggers
 A Home that keeps
 itself current.
 Ask for a small page and pin it to Home. Widgets show your data. A trigger can start a run on a schedule, or when a file arrives.
 The assistant proposes a page. You confirm it, then it is saved to your files.
 Home takes up to three page tiles and twelve widgets.
 A schedule trigger runs at most once every five minutes.
 Pages and Triggers are off for new users until an admin turns them on.
 In development builds, coming in the next release.
