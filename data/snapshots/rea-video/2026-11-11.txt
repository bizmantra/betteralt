 Read a complete example ↗
 Do I need editing experience? Start with the example frames, shot list, and steps. They explain the approach; making your own video may still require learning filming or editing tools.
 Does it create the finished video? Not currently. rea-video analyzes a reference and provides a production plan. You use filming, generation, and editing tools to make the final video.
 What do I need? Can I upload here? You need a local video file and an AI assistant that can run command-line tools. This website offers examples and guides, not browser uploads. The docs explain setup.
 Is it free? The rea-video core is open source and free to obtain. Your AI assistant or model provider may charge separately, depending on the service you choose.
 Will it recover the exact original method? No guarantee. It infers techniques from visible evidence and flags uncertainty. The plan is not the creator’s original project file or prompt.
