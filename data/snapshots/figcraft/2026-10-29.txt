Highlights
 Interface design
 What&#x27;s inside
.
 01
 Familiar by design
 The toolbar, layers panel, Design panel, menus and shortcuts sit where your hands expect them. Productive on day one, no retraining.
 02
 Layouts that build themselves
 Auto layout in both directions with wrap and grid, padding, gaps and Hug, Fill and Fixed sizing, plus constraints and layout grids for everything else.
 03
 Components that scale
 Instances with overrides that survive edits, component sets with variants and properties, and styles and variables with modes for light and dark.
 04
 Real vector networks
 Pen and pencil, a shape builder, booleans whose operands stay editable, variable-width strokes and smooth corners.
 05
 Prototype and hand off
 Connections, triggers and animated transitions, a presentation view, and Dev Mode with measurements, annotations and generated CSS, HTML, React, SwiftUI and Compose code.
 06
 Agent-ready
 Every menu item, tool and gesture runs one of 400+ commands, so the CLI, a JSON control channel and an MCP server can do anything you can click.
