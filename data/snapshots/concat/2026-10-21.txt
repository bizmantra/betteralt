 A tour of the window.
 Four panes, one screenshot, nothing mocked up. Scroll and the picture follows.
 01
 One window, four panes.
 Library, monitor, inspector and timeline. Every pane can be swapped, split or dragged somewhere else.
 02
 The Library.
 Media, Audio, Text, Stickers, Transitions, Effects, Filters, Adjustments and Templates as pages. Import footage, then single-click a filter to audition it on the monitor or drag it onto a lane.
 03
 The monitor.
 Scrub, play, step frames. Drag a title or a clip around in the picture; its position, scale and rotation follow. Quality switches between full, half and quarter.
 04
 The inspector.
 Everything about the selected clip: Text, Effects, Adjust, sound. Font, weight, size, colour, shadow and a background plate for titles, with keyframes a diamond away.
 05
 The timeline.
 Several tracks, several timelines per project. The tray under the tabs holds split, the razor, the magnetic switch, Text to Speech, Record Voiceover and Generate Captions.
 Built for the edits people make every day.
 Captions, voices, titles and effects on a multi-track timeline, rendered by a native Rust engine with a GPU compositor.
 Auto-captions, offline.
 Local Whisper transcribes on your machine and puts styled captions on the timeline, one title per phrase or per sentence.
 How captions work 
 Text to speech and voice cloning.
 Built-in speakers that are quick, or any voice from a few seconds of a recording. The result lands on the timeline.
 Generate a voice 
 Background removal.
 A person, an object, or paint the mask yourself with a smart brush. No green screen needed.
 Remove a background 
 Keyframes with a curve editor.
 Position, scale, rotation, opacity, volume and effect parameters. Ease in, ease out, or draw the curve.
 Animate anything 
 Titles that look designed.
 Fonts, stroke, shadow and a background plate, with presets to start from. Style many titles at once.
 Add titles 
 4K export, 10-bit, no watermark.
 H.264, HEVC and AV1 up to 4K 60. Hardware encoders where the machine has them. A bitrate that starts at what YouTube asks for, so the size shown is the size written.
 Export a video 
 Also: 100 effects, filters and transitions, chroma key, colour wheels and curves, one-switch voice cleanup, multi-timeline projects, stickers and shapes. Every feature, on GitHub

 Is Concat really free? 
 Yes. No watermark, no account, no paid tier, and the code is AGPL-3.0. Sponsorship is what stands in for a subscription, and it is optional.
 Does anything leave my machine? 
 No. Editing, captions, voices and cutout all run locally. The optional models download once from Settings and work offline after that. Logs stay on disk unless you attach them to an issue yourself.
 Where is the text-to-speech button? 
 On the timeline tray, the row of tools under the timeline tabs: the Text to Speech button opens the speech panel. The first time, download a voice model from Settings › Speech.
 The text-to-speech guide 
 Windows SmartScreen stops the installer. 
 The Windows builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen asks before the first run: click More info, then Run anyway. Where Smart App Control is on, Windows refuses unsigned programs outright.
 The code signing policy 
 macOS says the app is damaged or cannot be opened. 
 The macOS builds are unsigned. Move Concat to Applications, then clear the quarantine flag once:
 xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Concat.app 
 How do I install it on iPhone or iPad? 
 iOS is in beta and is not on the App Store. Download the IPA from the picker and install it by sideloading.
 What hardware do I need? 
 Minimum: any 64-bit processor from 2013 or later, 4 GB of RAM and 500 MB of storage, with no GPU required. For smooth 1080p editing and quick 4K exports: 6 cores or more, a GPU with Metal, DirectX 12 or Vulkan, and 16 GB of RAM.
