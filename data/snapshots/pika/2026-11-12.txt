 FAQ 
 How much does Pika cost?
 Pika has a Free plan. Starter is $8/month billed annually or $10/month billed monthly; Creator is $28/month billed annually or $35/month billed monthly; Fancy is from $76/month billed annually or from $95/month billed monthly. Enterprise pricing is custom. Every plan includes all models.
 Does Pika’s Free plan have a watermark?
 No. Free plan exports have no watermark.
 Does Pika offer free generations?
 Yes, for a limited time. Creator and Fancy plans include free Pika H3 Flex generations every day. Sign in to see today&#x27;s count.
 How much does a Seedance 2.5 video cost on Pika?
 Seedance 2.5 is included on every paid Pika plan from $10/month. A 5-second 720p clip costs about 122 credits, around $2.03 on Starter.
 Which Pika plans include commercial use?
 Starter, Creator, Fancy include a commercial license.
 How do I cancel?
 Cancel any time from the Subscription page. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, then you move to the Free plan. Payments are non-refundable, so there is no refund for the rest of the period.
 Do unused credits roll over?
 Plan credits refresh each billing period and do not roll over. Credits you buy as top-ups carry over and expire 365 days after purchase.
 Can I buy credits without changing plans?
 Yes. Top up from Credits on any plan, including Free — a top-up is a one-off purchase and does not start a subscription.
 What happens when I change plans?
 An upgrade applies immediately. A downgrade is scheduled for the start of your next billing period, and you can undo it until then.
 What models does Pika include?
 Pika is an AI video, image and audio platform. It runs Pika’s own models, Pika H3 Flex (open weights) and Pika 2.5, alongside Seedance 2.5, Kling 4.0, Veo 3.1, Wan 3.0, MiniMax H3 and Gemini Omni. Create and edit with multiple models in one place.
 What happened to Basic, Standard, Pro and Unlimited?
 Basic, Standard, Pro and Unlimited are legacy individual plan names. Current individual plans are Free, Starter, Creator and Fancy; Enterprise has custom pricing. For an existing legacy subscription, check your account’s Subscription page for its terms.
