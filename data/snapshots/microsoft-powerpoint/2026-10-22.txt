 01/ 
 Where can I use Microsoft PowerPoint?
 Anywhere you need to stay productive—on desktop (Windows and MacOS), mobile and tablets (iOS and Android) or online. PowerPoint works across devices so you can keep projects moving whether you’re at your desk or on the go. 
 02/ 
 Is there a free version of Microsoft PowerPoint?
 Yes, Microsoft PowerPoint for the web is free for anyone to use online. Sign up or sign in
 using any email address. 
 03/ 
 Can I collaborate with other people in Microsoft PowerPoint?
 Absolutely. Real-time collaboration means fewer email attachments and faster feedback, so you can finalize work without delays.​ 
 04/ 
 In what languages is Microsoft PowerPoint available?
 PowerPoint supports multiple languages, helping you communicate clearly and confidently with clients, partners, or teams worldwide.  Learn more
