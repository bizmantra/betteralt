 OpenMotion
 OpenMotion is a full-stack agentic video studio and an open-source alternative to Claude Motion and CapCut . Upload a reference video or paste a social video link to recreate it with your own brand kit and copy .
 🎬 Full-stack video recreation. Code video with HTML. Your agent breaks down the reference, rebuilds its shots and transitions, and generates missing assets. Edit the timing in Studio and render an MP4 from the same project.
 🔑 BYOA — Bring Your Own Agent. BYOK — Bring Your Own Keys. OpenMotion is free and open source. Recreate videos in your local agent
 with your own API keys. Supported agents include Claude Code, Codex, and WorkBuddy.
 🧩 OpenMotion introduces Motion.md , a Markdown format for shot order, timing and transitions. Use it to share how a brand film works, shot by shot.
 🎛️ Images, video, music and voice. Create artwork with GPT Image 2.6, add a shot with Seedance 2.5, score it with Suno and narrate it with Eleven v4. Explore the models and providers
.

 ✨ Why OpenMotion
 1. A full-stack agentic video studio
 Code video with HTML. OpenMotion uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript to describe scenes, typography, layout, transitions and timing. Recorded footage, generated clips, images, music and voice-over become media inside the composition. The same project opens in Studio and renders to MP4.
 Write it. Watch it. Change it. Render it. Ask your agent to replace the product, tighten the hook, add a presenter, change the captions or try another ending. It edits the project source; you review the result in the visual editor. Local MCP clients use the project tools, while agents with shell access can follow the Skills and pinned CLI workflow.  Projects and versions
 From a reference clip to an editable remake
 Break down the reference. Pick a sequence. Record its shot order, transitions and sound cues in Motion.md, and mark what you want to replace.
 Rebuild the shots. Your agent rebuilds the HTML scenes using your brand kit, copy and visuals, then arranges them on an editable timeline in Studio.
 Compare the cuts. Watch the same moment in the original and the remake. Adjust the timing, movement and sound, then play the sequence through.
 Export and share. Render the MP4 and keep the HTML source and media. To share a project others can adapt, include its Motion.md, a preview and assets you can redistribute. Project contribution checklist

 3. 300+ Motion.md guides for video recreation
 OpenMotion introduces Motion.md : a Markdown format for shot order, timing, transitions and the assets you can replace. The online library brings reference videos and their motion guides into the same detail page.
 The Brand Kit section records your identity, voice and approved product facts; the Design section can reference an independent DESIGN.md for visual rules. Keep the reference’s motion and pacing while making the result recognizably yours.
 To share an industry or brand system, use the Motion Package v0.2 draft
 and authoring toolkit
. A package combines the design and motion documents with materials, declared dependencies and optional SKILL.md . The included recipe demonstrates two content presets; automatic Studio import and rendered fidelity remain separate from package validation.

 System architecture
 Editing the timeline in Studio and asking your agent to change a shot both update the same HTML project. The local TypeScript service manages media and revisions. A browser-based rendering engine exports the selected version to MP4.
 How the modules work together
 Edits made through the project tools include a revision check so an outdated request cannot overwrite newer work. Generated media enters the project when you select it. Each export uses the version saved when the job starts. The exporter checks the file specifications, and you can play the cut to review the picture, sound and timing.

 About
 The open-source Claude Motion & CapCut alternative. Turn a reference video into editable motion graphics with your brand kit. Export MP4s for YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok. A full-stack agentic video studio with 300+ Motion.md guides and support for 22 agents, including Claude Code, Codex, DSH, and WorkBuddy.
