 Questions before you pay
 Is AI usage included? +
 Pro includes $10 and Max includes $20 of Claude credit every month. Beyond included credit, bring your own Claude or ChatGPT account, or an API key. Model usage on your account is billed by your provider.
 Can I talk to my bots on a call? +
 Yes, on every plan, the free app included. Live voice calls use your own OpenAI key: add it in Settings → API keys, and OpenAI bills each call to it. The voice included with a Cloud plan is for your bots&#x27; spoken replies.
 What is a computer-hour? +
 One hour of a cloud computer your bots use, with a live desktop you can watch or take over. A small computer counts as half an hour. Allowances reset on the 1st of each month (UTC).
 Do I need to keep my laptop on? +
 Not on a paid plan. Your bots run on your own cloud machine, which stays on around the clock on every Cloud plan, and the phone apps reach them while your laptop is closed. On Free, bots work while your computer is on.
 I could run the free app on my own server. Why pay? +
 You can: the app is open source and always will be. A Cloud plan is for when you would rather not run a server yourself. It comes with cloud computers your bots can drive, voice, automations that run while you sleep, phone access, and updates we look after.
 What happens on the setup call? +
 Pro and Max include 30 minutes with us after you subscribe. We pick the first jobs with you and get those bots running, so you leave the call with work already happening.
 Can I cancel any time? +
 Yes. Plans are billed monthly with no contract, and you can cancel any time from the billing page.
