Frequently asked questions
What if I am a student or teacher?
We’re on a mission to shape a brighter future by empowering the next generation of storytellers. Our goal is to provide our writing assistant free of charge to over 5,000 schools by the end of 2026.
This initiative allows primary and secondary school students to enhance their writing skills with the support of our tool, at no cost to educators. To take part, your teacher needs to invite you to join ProWritingAid for Education. Here’s how to ask your teacher to sign up for ProWritingAid for Education and join us in our quest to nurture and develop young, creative minds.
What is your refund policy?
If you are unsatisfied with your ProWritingAid yearly or lifetime paid plan, please contact us within three days of purchase at hello@prowritingaid.com to request a refund. Please note that canceling your subscription will not automatically generate a refund—you’ll still need to contact us within three days.
Refunds are not possible for monthly paid plans. If you would like to stop future payments, you can do this at any time by going to your user profile and turning off auto-renew.
Purchases of Story Credits are nonrefundable. However, if you have purchased Story Credits at the same time as a new subscription and cancel your subscription under the three-day money-back guarantee, we will refund you for any unused credits.
Do you offer a student discount?
Yes. We offer an exclusive 25% discount for university and college students. All you need is a verified Student Beans account to unlock your discount code.
How secure is my work?
With ProWritingAid, your work is absolutely secure.
We never keep your work or any text entered into our editor. We run thousands of analysis requests every day. The data is encrypted and sent to our servers to be processed, but it is never stored on these servers.
Your documents are created and uploaded to your account, but you are the only one who has access to them. 
For more information, please read our privacy policy here.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time by going to your account and clicking "Cancel subscription." Your account will remain active until the renewal date.
What is the benefit of paid vs Free?
With a paid license, you can run our line editing reports on an unlimited word count, and get unlimited Rephrases. You’ll also get more Sparks, Chapter Critiques, Creative Nudges, Premium style suggestions, and exclusive discounts on Story Credits.
What is the benefit of Premium Pro vs Premium?
With a ProWritingAid Premium Pro license, you unlock higher usage limits for extended editing features, including Chapter Critique, Creative Nudges, and Sparks. You also unlock our best prices for Story Credits, which provide in-depth analysis of your whole manuscript.
Can I use ProWritingAid on more than one computer?
Yes. Our licenses are for individual users, so as long as you’re the only person using the account, you can install ProWritingAid on several computers. However, if other people are using a shared computer, they will need to buy their own license.
How many words can I check at one time?
With a free account, you will only receive suggestions for 500 words at a time. With a Premium or Premium Pro license, you can use ProWritingAid with documents of any length.
What languages can I use ProWritingAid with?
Currently, ProWritingAid is only available in English. This includes General English, British English, US English, Australian English, and Canadian English.
Will my license auto-renew?
Yes. If you purchase a monthly or annual Premium or Premium Pro license, your purchase will renew automatically on the date of your initial purchase using the card you have on file.
Our lifetime licenses are a one-time payment, so once purchased, you will not be charged again.
Can Free users buy Story Credits?
Yes. Free users can purchase Story Credits to run Manuscript Analysis, Virtual Beta Reader, Character Analysis, Plot Analysis, or Marketability Analysis. Upgrading to Premium or Premium Pro gets you special discounts on credits.
What is the difference between all story editing features?
Chapter Critique gives you instant feedback on a short story or single chapter of a longer story. It’s a great way to get instant guidance on what’s working well and where you can improve.
Manuscript Analysis gives you in-depth, actionable feedback on your whole manuscript, prioritized by how critical each area is to the success of your story. Running an analysis on your full manuscript gives you the most comprehensive developmental feedback, as it takes your whole story into consideration.
Plot Analysis checks your story against the same three-act structure used in bestselling novels to help you raise tension, deepen arcs, and strengthen momentum. Ideal for first drafts or testing story outlines.
Character Analysis helps you understand how your characters are working across your manuscript—revealing where they connect, where they fade, and how to make them more compelling on the page.
Virtual Beta Reader gives you a reader’s perspective on your whole story, with detailed insights into how they will experience your story—what they’ll feel, where they’ll be hooked, and when their interest might fade. It helps you to identify what’s working well and where you can make changes to your story before you share it with beta readers or editors.
Marketability Analysis helps you understand your book’s potential, identify your target audience, and take confident steps toward traditional or self-publication. It also provides you with ready-made toolkit documents, templates, and promotional assets—all purposefully designed for your book.
