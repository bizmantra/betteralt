Highlights
 Video editor
 What&#x27;s inside
.
 01
 A timeline you already know
 Source and program monitors, three-point editing, every trim mode, and J/K/L dynamic trimming.
 02
 Frame-exact time
 An integer timebase keeps 23.976 and 29.97 drop-frame exact. No drift, no rounding, no mystery frames.
 03
 Its own codecs
 No FFmpeg inside. Native H.264, HEVC, ProRes, VP9, AAC and Opus, with bit-exact decoders.
 04
 Color grading
 Curves, color wheels, HSL secondaries, LUTs, HDR and log workflows, plus scopes to check your work.
 05
 Effects and keyframes
 About 55 video effects and 30 transitions, keyframes with value and velocity graphs, and a linear-light GPU compositor.
 06
 Broadcast-ready audio
 EBU R128 loudness meters, a mixer with automation, and native DSP effects.
