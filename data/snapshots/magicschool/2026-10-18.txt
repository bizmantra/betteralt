 If my school or district doesn’t have MagicSchool Enterprise, can I still access all educator features?
 Yes, teachers can use MagicSchool independently through Free or Plus plans. Enterprise adds district-level management and visibility, but classroom tools remain available to individual educators.
 ‍
 What tools does MagicSchool provide for district-level visibility and usage monitoring?
 Enterprise includes district dashboards that provide visibility into adoption and overall usage patterns across schools. District leaders can manage access, support implementation, and ensure AI use aligns with local policies and instructional priorities.
 How does pricing compare to other AI platforms?
 Many AI tools aren’t built with the needs of teachers and students in mind and are priced beyond their reach, with Microsoft Copilot being priced at $25 a month and ChatGPT Plus priced at $20 per month . In addition, both are not customized for education and do not provide safe student access. Our pricing meets the needs of school and district budgets. Our yearly cost is over 50% lower than both Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT Plus with more value including student usage. 
 How much does it cost for a school or district license to MagicSchool Enterprise? How much for an individual subscription to MagicSchool Plus?
 For school and district Enterprise licenses , we offer volume-based discounts, additional features listed on the chart above, unparalleled customer support, and much more. Request a quote now!
