 FAQ
 Choose your plan according to your monetization and scale
 Which plan should I choose?
 Start with Free to try music generation and editing. Choose Creator for your own personal, non-monetized content. Pro is for monetized content and digital ads. Choose Ultra for client and agency work, music in apps or games, or tracks up to 60 minutes.
 What’s the difference between Creator and Pro?
 Creator includes 100 generations, 50 downloads and 10 Track Editor trial versions per month, with tracks up to 10 minutes. Pro includes 500 generations, unlimited downloads, the full Track Editor and tracks up to 25 minutes. Pro also adds monetization and digital-ad rights, plus a one-time Fuse + Cast Plus bonus.
 What does Ultra add to Pro?
 Ultra includes 1,000 generations per month, tracks up to 60 minutes and the same full Track Editor. It adds rights for client and agency work and music in apps or games, plus a one-time Fuse + Cast Max bonus.
 How is AI Music Chat different from Text → Music?
 Text2Music generates music from a single prompt. AI Music Chat better interprets the context of your brief, offers two demos to compare, and lets you develop your chosen direction through feedback without repeating the original request.
 Can I use AI Music Chat for free?
 Yes, after signing up. Free and paid plans use the same AI Music Chat system. Free chat access has a limited allowance and a 5-hour cooldown once it is used up. Your monthly track and download limits still apply.
 Does AI Music Chat include every editing feature?
 Available edits depend on your plan. Free includes 3 Full Track Editor trial edits per month, and Creator includes 10. These allowances also apply when you request those edits in Chat. Pro and Ultra include Full Track Editor access within their generation limits.
 How do Chat demos and track generations count toward my limits?
 The initial pair of demos uses your chat allowance without deducting track generations. Creating a standalone track or a new edited version uses one Render generation. Chat usage and monthly track and download limits are separate.
 Does the 5-hour cooldown reset my monthly limits?
 The cooldown restores free chat usage. Monthly track and download limits reset on their own schedule.
 Can I try the full Track Editor before paying?
 Yes. Registered Free users can create 3 trial versions per month. Creator includes 10 per month. You can change the structure, sections, stems, duration, BPM and key. Each generated trial version also uses one generation from your monthly allowance. Your original track stays unchanged.
 Can I use Mubert music commercially?
 Creator is intended for non-commercial content. Commercial usage and monetization require Pro or Ultra depending on the use case.
 Can I publish generated tracks to Spotify or other music streaming services?
 No. Mubert tracks cannot be redistributed as standalone music or registered in Content ID systems. They can be licensed as music used within eligible content and projects according to the selected plan.
 What happens if I cancel my subscription?
 You cannot use previously generated tracks in new projects after the subscription ends. Projects published while the relevant subscription was active can remain published and continue to be monetized according to their existing license.
 What is the Fuse + Cast bonus?
 Pro includes one-time access to Tools Plus; Ultra includes Tools Max. The bonus lasts 30 days with a monthly Render purchase or 90 days with an annual purchase. One Tools plan covers both Fuse and Cast, with its own features, credits, limits and licence, separate from Render.
 When does the bonus start, and will I be charged?
 The bonus starts when you activate it. There is no activation deadline, extra payment or additional payment-card requirement. The bonus does not automatically start or renew a paid Tools subscription.
 Will renewing or upgrading Render give me another bonus?
 The bonus is once per account. Renewing Render does not restart it.
If you upgrade from Pro to Ultra before activating the bonus, it changes to Max with the duration for your selected billing period. If the bonus is already active, Plus changes to Max for the remaining time; the end date stays the same. Upgrading after an expired bonus does not give you another one.
 Do annual Render plans have annual generation limits?
 No. Generation and download allowances are monthly on annual plans too. Track Editor trial allowances are also monthly. The Fuse + Cast bonus is a single 90-day period, not a monthly benefit.
 Can I release the tracks on Spotify or register them in Content ID?
 No. These plans do not allow standalone music releases, redistribution through music stocks or libraries, or Content ID registration. They license music for use within permitted content and projects.
 What if I receive a copyright claim?
 Mubert music is royalty-free, but false-positive claims may occasionally happen. The license certificate supplied with the downloaded track can be used to confirm your right to use it.
 Are payments refundable?
 Payments are generally non-refundable because access to digital content is provided immediately. If a user selected the wrong plan and wants to upgrade, they can contact Support.
