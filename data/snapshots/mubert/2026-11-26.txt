 FAQ
 Choose your plan according to your monetization and scale
 Which plan should I choose?
 Choose by the tools, volume and rights you need. Free lets you try music generation and audio separation. Creator adds higher limits and WAV exports. Pro adds more processing, the full Track Editor and commercial rights for Mubert-generated music. Ultra adds the largest allowances and rights for client work, apps and games. For uploaded audio, compare processing minutes and maximum file length; publishing rights depend on the recording you upload.
 What’s the difference between Creator and Pro?
 Creator includes 100 generations, 50 music downloads and 10 editor trial versions per month, with generated tracks up to 10 minutes. It also includes 200 audio-processing minutes, uploaded files up to 20 minutes and four-stem separation. 
 Pro includes 500 generations, unlimited music downloads, generated tracks up to 25 minutes, the full Track Editor, 600 processing minutes, uploaded files up to 30 minutes and six-stem separation. Both plans include WAV exports. Pro also adds monetization and digital-ad rights for Mubert-generated music and a one-time Tools Plus bonus for Fuse
 and Cast
.
 What does Ultra add to Pro?
 Ultra increases monthly generations to 1,000 and audio processing to 1,400 minutes. Both generated tracks and uploaded files can be up to 60 minutes long. It also adds rights for client and agency work and music in finished apps or games, plus a one-time Fuse
 + Cast
 Max bonus.
 How is AI Music Chat different from Text → Music?
 Text2Music generates music from a single prompt. AI Music Chat better interprets the context of your brief, offers two demos to compare, and lets you develop your chosen direction through feedback without repeating the original request.
 Can I use AI Music Chat for free?
 Yes, after signing up. Ambassador and paid plans use the same AI Music Chat system. On Ambassador, chat access has a limited allowance and a 5-hour cooldown once it is used up. Your monthly track and download limits still apply.
 Does AI Music Chat include every editing feature?
 Available edits depend on your plan. Ambassador includes 3 Full Track Editor trial edits per month, and Creator includes 10. These allowances also apply when you request those edits in Chat. Pro and Ultra include Full Track Editor access within their generation limits.
 How do Chat demos and track generations count toward my limits?
 The initial pair of demos uses your chat allowance without deducting track generations. Creating a standalone track or a new edited version uses one Render generation. Chat usage and monthly track and download limits are separate.
 Does the 5-hour cooldown reset my monthly limits?
 The cooldown restores free chat usage. Monthly track and download limits reset on their own schedule.
 Are Vocal Remover and Stem Splitter included in my plan?
 Yes. Monthly processing allowances are 20 minutes on Free , 200 on Creator , 600 on Pro and 1,400 on Ultra . All plans include four-stem separation; Pro and Ultra also include six stems. Free exports MP3; paid plans also support WAV exports. Processed results remain available for 24 hours on Free and seven days on paid plans. Uploaded source files are removed from active processing storage after processing.
 Can I try the full Track Editor before paying?
 Yes. On Ambassador, registered users can create 3 trial versions per month. Creator includes 10 per month. You can change the structure, sections, stems, duration, BPM and key. Each generated trial version also uses one generation from your monthly allowance. Your original track stays unchanged.
 What is the Fuse + Cast bonus?
 Pro includes one-time access to Tools Plus; Ultra includes Tools Max. The bonus lasts 30 days with a monthly Render purchase or 90 days with an annual purchase. One Tools plan covers both Fuse
 and Cast
 , with its own features, credits, limits and licence, separate from Render.
 Can I use Mubert music commercially?
 Creator is intended for non-commercial content. Commercial usage and monetization require Pro or Ultra depending on the use case.
 Can I publish generated tracks to Spotify or other music streaming services?
 No. Mubert tracks cannot be redistributed as standalone music or registered in Content ID systems. They can be licensed as music used within eligible content and projects according to the selected plan.
 What happens if I cancel my subscription?
 You cannot use previously generated tracks in new projects after the subscription ends. Projects published while the relevant subscription was active can remain published and continue to be monetized according to their existing license.
 When does the bonus start, and will I be charged?
 The bonus starts when you activate it. There is no activation deadline, extra payment or additional payment-card requirement. The bonus does not automatically start or renew a paid Tools subscription.
 Will renewing or upgrading Render give me another bonus?
 The bonus is once per account. Renewing Render does not restart it.
If you upgrade from Pro to Ultra before activating the bonus, it changes to Max with the duration for your selected billing period. If the bonus is already active, Plus changes to Max for the remaining time; the end date stays the same. Upgrading after an expired bonus does not give you another one.
 Do annual Render plans have annual generation limits?
 No. Music generations, music downloads, editor trial versions and audio processing allowances are monthly on annual plans too. The Fuse + Cast bonus is a single 90-day period.
 What if I receive a copyright claim?
 Mubert music is royalty-free, but false-positive claims may occasionally happen. The license certificate supplied with the downloaded track can be used to confirm your right to use it.
 Are payments refundable?
 Payments are generally non-refundable because access to digital content is provided immediately. If a user selected the wrong plan and wants to upgrade, they can contact Support.
 Do stem downloads use my music download allowance?
 No. The music download allowance applies to Mubert music. Vocal Remover and Stem Splitter use processing minutes instead. Once a result is ready, you can download its parts or export them again during the storage period without using extra processing minutes or music downloads.
