 FAQs
 How does Intercom pricing work?
 Intercom pricing has two components:
 Seats: You pay per teammate based on your plan (Essential, Advanced, Expert)
 Usage: You pay for what you use (e.g. Fin outcomes, messaging channels)
 All plans include access to Intercom and Fin AI Agent. Want a tailored estimate? Use the pricing calculator
.
 How is Fin AI Agent priced?
 Fin is priced at $0.99 per outcome .
 An outcome is counted when:
 A customer confirms their issue is resolved, or
 They don’t ask for more help after Fin responds, or
 Fin completes a workflow (Procedure), including handoffs
 You’re only charged once per conversation, even if multiple questions are answered.
 Can I use Fin with my existing helpdesk?
 Yes. Fin can be used with tools like Salesforce and others.
 Pricing: 
 $0.99 per outcome
 Minimum monthly commitment applies (e.g. 50 outcomes)
 No seat costs
 No setup, integration, or platform fees
 What plans does Intercom offer?
 Intercom offers three plans:
 Essential
 Advanced
 Expert
 Your plan determines:
 Seat price
 Feature access
 Included capabilities (e.g. Lite seats on higher tiers)
 What is a seat (Full vs Lite)?
 Full seat: Paid. Required for teammates who interact with customers and use core features.
 Lite seat: Free (on Advanced/Expert). Limited access for internal collaboration.
 Only Full seats can send messages or fully use features like outbound content.
 Are there additional usage charges?
 Yes. Beyond seats, pricing is usage-based for certain features:
 Fin AI Agent: $0.99 per outcome
 WhatsApp: Charged per message sent (varies by market/message type)
 SMS: Charged per message (varies by region)
 Email campaigns: Charged per bulk email sent
 Phone: Usage-based (varies)
 Inbound support (e.g. responding to customer emails) is included.
 What is Proactive Support Plus?
 An optional add-on for outbound engagement.
 $99/month includes 500 messages 
 Covers: Product Tours, Surveys, In-app messages, Mobile Push, Carousels
 Additional messages are charged per unit
 How much is Copilot?
 Copilot is an optional add-on at $29 per agent/month billed annually , or $35 per agent/month billed monthly, for unlimited usage.
 Copilot is a personal AI assistant in the Inbox that helps your team resolve conversations faster. Every agent also gets 10 Copilot and 10 AI Auto-translation conversations free each month.
 What is the Pro add-on?
 Pro is an optional add-on at $100/month , which includes analysis of 500 conversations/month and 6,000 credits per month.
 Pro is a set of advanced AI features for managing and improving your support operation. It includes Operator, an agent for customer operations, plus features that help you analyze performance, monitor quality, identify trends, and continuously improve Fin.
 Credits are the unit used to measure and charge for Operator usage. Every Pro workspace receives monthly credits, and the number of credits included scales with conversation volume. If you use more credits than what’s included in Pro, you can buy additional credits at $0.01 per credit. Simple actions use fewer credits, while more complex work, like analyzing large sets of conversations, building automation, or testing changes, uses more. You can track usage, set alerts, and use hard limits to control spend.
 Do I need a contract?
 No.
 Monthly or annual self-serve plans are available (Essential, Advanced)
 Contracts are optional and required for: 
 Enterprise needs
 Premier Support / onboarding
 Custom terms
 What’s the minimum to get started?
 1 Full seat minimum
 No required platform fees beyond that
 Usage (e.g. Fin, messaging) is pay-as-you-go
 This makes it easy to start small and scale.
 Is there a free trial?
 Yes.
 14-day free trial
 No credit card required
 Includes all features, Fin, and add-ons
 You only pay once you choose a plan and add payment details.
 How do I change my plan or seats?
 You can:
 Upgrade/downgrade plans
 Add/remove seats
 Add or remove add-ons
 Handled directly in your workspace (or via Sales if under contract). Downgrades typically take effect at renewal.
 Are there discounts available?
 Yes.
 Startups: Up to 93% off + 1 year of Fin free (via Early Stage program
)
 Enterprise discounts may be available via Sales
