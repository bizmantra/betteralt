 Pricing
 Is it really not priced per seat?
 Yes. You pay one price for the workspace. Each plan has a ceiling on people and storage, and you only move up when you go past it.
 What happens when we grow past our plan?
 Nothing is deleted and nobody is removed. You can’t add more people or files until you move to a bigger plan or make room.
 Can I cancel any time?
 Yes, in the workspace’s settings. Your plan stays until the end of the period you paid for, then the workspace goes back to Duo. You can get a full refund within 14 days of your first payment — refund policy
