 What is OpenFilm?
 A film is a folder of web pages, cut together by one more: film.html. Your agent writes them; you change them by hand.
 Read docs
 Open standard One spec for every film, so any video can be copied, remixed and rebuilt.
 Any agent Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or anything that runs a command.
 Edit by hand Click a word, a color or a shot in Studio and change it.
 Any web tech Every shot is a web page: CSS, SVG, canvas, WebGL.
 Footage Your clips cut on the beat, with titles, tracking and B-roll.
 Exact renders Every frame in parallel, up to 4K.
 Local first Films are folders on your disk; voice, music and images from the services you choose.

 FAQ
 Something else? Ask on GitHub
.
 What is OpenFilm? An open source video agent. Your coding agent writes the film as web pages, using OpenFilm's manual and tools, and you watch and edit it in OpenFilm Studio.
 How do I use OpenFilm? Add the skill to your coding agent with npx skills add openfilm/openfilm , then ask it for a video. Without skills, ask it to run npx openfilm and make a video; or run npx openfilm open in a folder yourself.
 Do I need an account? No. Making, editing and rendering films is free and happens on your computer. To generate voice-over, music, pictures and video, connect services such as ElevenLabs, OpenAI or Google with your own keys, or sign in to an OpenFilm account to use every model from one place.
 Which agents work with OpenFilm? Any agent that can run a command: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, OpenCode, Windsurf, Cline, Amp, Devin and more.
 How much does OpenFilm cost? OpenFilm itself is free. With your own keys, each service bills you directly. With the OpenFilm service, what you make comes out of your account’s credits, and every model’s price is shown beside its official one on your account’s Pricing page.
 Is OpenFilm open source? Yes, under the MIT license.
