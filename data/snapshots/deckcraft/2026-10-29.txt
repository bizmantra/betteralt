Highlights
 Presentations & slide shows
 What&#x27;s inside
.
 01
 Master slides & themes
 Slide masters and eleven layouts with inheriting placeholders, eight original themes with font and colour schemes, sections, and presenter notes.
 02
 Vector shapes & drawing
 Over 150 preset shapes with adjustment handles, sticky connectors, curve booleans, gradient fills, and outline styling.
 03
 Transitions & animation
 Slide transitions including Morph, entrance, emphasis, exit and motion-path animations, with by-paragraph builds.
 04
 Presenter view
 Full-screen slide show delivery with speaker notes, upcoming slide previews, elapsed timers, rehearsal timings, and pen drawing.
 05
 Format compatibility
 Open and save presentation files (.pptx), export vector PDF slides and handouts, and render slide images.
 06
 Automated with MCP
 Over 200 commands scriptable from the CLI, a JSON control channel, and an MCP server for autonomous agents.
