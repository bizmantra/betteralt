 How usage-based pricing works
 Every plan includes a monthly amount of usage. Usage pays for new SEO data you look up: keyword research, backlinks, rank checks, site audits, and AI visibility checks. Each action costs what the data costs us plus a flat 28 %, rounded up to the nearest tenth of a cent, so a keyword search is about $0.05 and a rank check is a fraction of a cent.
 Your projects, saved results, exports, and Google Search Console data never use any. When you need more than your plan includes, buy extra usage or turn on auto-reload up to a monthly limit you set. Extra usage never expires.

 FAQ
 Is there a free trial? Yes. The free trial includes $0.50 of credits so you can test OpenSEO before subscribing. 
 What if I use all my credits for the month? Buy top-up credits at any time, or turn on auto-reload so OpenSEO buys them for you when you run low, up to a monthly limit you set. 
 What features use credits? Keyword research, backlinks, competitor data, site audits, rank tracking, and AI visibility research and tracking use credits. Your projects, settings, and any data already fetched don&#x27;t cost credits. 
 Do unused credits roll over? Top-up credits roll over indefinitely. The Usage Credits included with your plan reset each billing cycle. 
 Can I cancel anytime? Yes. Cancel from your billing portal at any time. Your access continues through the end of the current billing period. 
 Do I need a subscription or just usage credits? While top-up Usage Credits roll over and don&#x27;t expire, you need an active subscription in order to use OpenSEO.
